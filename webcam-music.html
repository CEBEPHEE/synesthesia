Synesthesia

Make music with your webcam

Your camera becomes an instrument: the music listens to what it sees, live, and every change lands on the beat.

Open Synesthesia, free →
  1. Press “Use camera”. Allow the camera when the browser asks. The picture never leaves your device.
  2. Choose what it follows. The picture (colours and shapes, steady) or your moves (dance and the music grows; stand still and it holds; raise your arms and the melody goes up).
  3. Show colours. Hold something colourful up to the camera; if it stays for about two bars, the key and chords change to match it.
  4. Record. Take a photo or record a video with the music, then save it or let the app compose a new soundtrack for it.

🔒 Everything happens in your browser tab: nothing is uploaded, no account is needed.

Questions

Can I make music with my webcam?

Yes. Press “Use camera”: choose whether the music follows the picture (colours held up to the camera change the key) or your moves (body and hand tracking: dance and the bass and drums come in, stand still and the music holds); every change lands on the beat. You can take photos or record videos with the music.

Are my photos or videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser tab: your photos, videos and camera picture never leave your device, and the page is technically blocked from sending them anywhere. There is no account and nothing is stored.
